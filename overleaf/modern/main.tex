\section{Profile}
Electrical engineering Ph.D.\ student building low-cost, low-power wireless systems. My research turns
the existing digital I/O pins of FPGAs into backscatter modulators so that ordinary Bluetooth
chipsets can receive BLE packets, with no added components. Prior work spans wireless links for
brain--computer interfaces, RF measurement automation, and antenna design.
